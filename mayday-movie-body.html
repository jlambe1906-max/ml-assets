1987. At the height of the Cold War, U.S. Navy pilot Lieutenant Troy “Assassin” Kelly is shot down deep inside Soviet territory. Ryan Reynolds and Kenneth Branagh star in the action-comedy from directors John Francis Daley and Jonathan Goldstein. Rated PG-13. 1h 51m.
